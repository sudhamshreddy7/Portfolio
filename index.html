How I approach payments systems

Payments platforms demand idempotency, auditability, and predictable performance under load. I focus on safe rollouts, strong observability, and clear contracts between services.

  • Async-first integrations: message-driven flows with structured acknowledgments (“received”, “validated”).
  • Throughput + latency: decouple services, isolate hot paths, tune SQL indexes + joins.
  • Cost + reliability: cross-region migrations and consolidation (e.g., multi-service → optimized Lambda).
  • Modernization: JDK 8→21 upgrades for performance and security posture.
